Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how the Email Threat Detection prototype may process information when you use the service.

1. Information submitted

When you upload an email file, the service processes its contents to generate threat-detection results and forensic findings. Emails can contain sender and recipient addresses, message content, URLs, attachments, and other identifying information.

2. Purpose of processing

Submitted files are used to run the application's detection rules, model analysis, and forensic processing and to return results to the user.

3. Storage and logs

Do not assume that uploaded data is never stored. Hosting providers, application logs, diagnostic systems, or future application changes may retain some information. The project team must verify actual storage, logging, backups, and retention settings before making a no-storage guarantee.

4. Third-party infrastructure

The application is hosted using Render. Its infrastructure and related services may process technical information such as connection data and server logs under their applicable policies.

5. Sensitive information

Please do not upload passwords, financial records, confidential business emails, or personal data unless you are authorized to do so and understand the risks of using this prototype.

6. Security and retention

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect the application, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. The project team should define and follow a retention and deletion policy for any stored data.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the project evolves. For questions, contact the project team through its official project or repository page.
