Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how SentinelMail, an email threat detection and forensic analysis prototype, may handle information submitted through the application.

1. Information you submit

When you upload an email file, it may contain email addresses, message content, headers, URLs, IP addresses and attachments or references to them. The application may extract information needed for threat detection and forensic analysis.

2. How information is used

Submitted information is processed to provide email threat analysis, suspicious-indicator detection and forensic results. Depending on the enabled modules, some indicators may be checked using external services.

3. Storage and external services

Data retention, server logs, investigation history and external service processing depend on the application's implementation and hosting configuration. Do not assume uploaded emails are automatically deleted or that no data is logged.

4. Sensitive information

Do not upload confidential, personal, financial or otherwise sensitive emails unless you are authorized to do so and understand how this deployment handles the data.

5. Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used, but no online application can guarantee absolute security.

6. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the prototype changes. For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the official project contact channel.

Before publishing, verify the actual data retention, logging and external-service behavior of your deployment.
